MeTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

MeTube is a private Android app used by one family. It is not distributed to the public. This page explains what the app does with information.

What the app stores

  • On the phone: the channels and videos a parent approved or rejected, blocked keywords, settings, a hashed (scrambled) parent PIN and recovery code, and any video files the parent imported.
  • Google account: if sync is turned on, the app stores the Google account's ID and email address on the phone to know which account it belongs to.

Google Drive

If a parent turns on sync, MeTube asks for permission to use the app data folder in the child's Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). This is a hidden folder only MeTube can read or write. The app saves the parent's decisions and settings there so they match on every family device. It cannot see any other Drive files. Video files are never uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No servers: the app does not send information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • No passwords are collected; sign-in is handled by Google.
  • Information is not sold or shared.

Deleting data

In the PIN-protected parent area, Delete all data removes everything the app stored on the phone and, optionally, the synced copy in Google Drive. Drive access can also be removed at any time in the Google account's security settings under third-party apps.
