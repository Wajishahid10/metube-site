MeTube

MeTube is a private, family-only Android app that gives a young child a small, parent-approved video catalogue. It is not published on any app store.

  • A parent approves every channel and video before the child can see it.
  • Parent controls are protected by a PIN.
  • Optional sync keeps settings the same across the family's devices, using a private app folder in the family's own MeTube Google account.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
